Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 11 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KingSPi (“we”, “us”), the independent developer of the mobile game Spellbun (the “Game”), collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you play Spellbun on iOS or Android.

By installing or using the Game, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the Game. Our Terms & Conditions also apply.

1. Who we are

Spellbun is published by KingSPi as an independent developer. We process personal data as the controller for the Game. We do not sell personal information.

2. Information we collect

Spellbun does not require you to type an email or password to start playing. An anonymous account is created automatically so progress can sync. You may optionally link Apple or Google. We do not ask for your precise location, contacts, photos, SMS, or microphone.

Account and identifiers

  • Anonymous account. When cloud features are available, we create an anonymous session with our backend (Supabase). That session has a unique user id.
  • Player id. The Game generates a random player id on your device. We use it to attach your save, Arcade scores, analytics, and crash reports.
  • Linked sign-in (optional). If you use Link account with Apple or Google, the identity provider shares an identifier with our auth backend so story progress, BunBun coins, and inventory can survive reinstall. Depending on the provider and your settings, this may include an email address or a private relay email (Sign in with Apple).

Gameplay and cloud save

  • On device. Progress is stored locally (for example story progress, wallet, settings, and language).
  • Cloud save. The same progress payload is synced to our database so you can restore after reinstall once an account is linked or the anonymous session is still valid.
  • Language. English or French, used for the UI, dictionary, and Arcade boards.

Arcade leaderboards

If you play Arcade, we may store and display:

  • your high score and when it was set;
  • a player name you choose (up to 16 characters);
  • a country code (two letters, or a placeholder if none is available);
  • the language of that board (English or French).

Other players can see name, country, and score. Do not put personal information in your player name.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (for example Remove ads and BunBun coin packs) are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We receive product identifiers and whether an entitlement such as ads-free is active. We do not receive your full payment card number. Restore purchases talks to Apple or Google, not to our cloud save.

Analytics

We use GameAnalytics to understand how the Game is played. Events currently include story and Arcade start, complete, fail (including quit), and sinks such as shuffle, lock, hint, and shop purchases. GameAnalytics may collect device and advertising identifiers, IP address, device type, OS version, country/region derived from IP, session data, and the player id we attach. This is used to operate and improve the Game, not to sell your data.

Crash and error reports

We use Sentry for native crashes and engine/script/shader errors (not ordinary warnings or print logs). Reports may include stack traces, device and OS information, app version, and the player id. They can include log snippets needed to diagnose a crash.

Technical data from our hosts

Our backend and the providers above automatically process technical data such as IP address, timestamps, and request metadata in order to deliver the service, keep it secure, and prevent abuse.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect government ID, precise GPS, contacts, photos, health data, or payment card PAN. We do not run a public social network inside Spellbun beyond Arcade names and scores.

3. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • run the Game, including cloud save, account linking, Arcade records, and restoring purchases through the stores;
  • remember your progress, language, and entitlements;
  • diagnose crashes and improve stability;
  • understand feature use (for example which stages are failed or which shop items are bought);
  • prevent cheating, abuse, and technical attacks;
  • comply with law, store policies, and valid legal requests;
  • communicate about privacy or account requests you send us.

Where privacy law requires a “legal basis,” we rely on: performing the contract to provide the Game; our legitimate interests in security, analytics, and debugging (balanced against your rights); your consent where required (for example certain advertising or identifier uses); and legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We share information with processors who help us operate Spellbun, only as needed for their role:

  • Supabase — authentication (anonymous, Apple, Google), cloud save, Arcade leaderboards, and account deletion.
  • Apple — App Store distribution, In-App Purchase, Sign in with Apple, and related fraud/receipt checks.
  • Google — Google Play distribution, Play Billing, Google sign-in, and related fraud/receipt checks.
  • GameAnalytics — gameplay analytics as described above.
  • Sentry — crash and error reporting as described above.

Other players see Arcade display data you submit. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect the Game or other players, or in connection with a sale or transfer of the Game (the new operator would still have to honour this Policy or notify you of changes). We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising in the current Game.

Those providers have their own privacy policies. You should read them if you want detail on how they process data as independent controllers or processors:

  • Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • GameAnalytics Privacy Policy
  • Sentry Privacy Policy

5. Advertising

The current version of Spellbun does not show third-party ads. The shop includes a Remove ads purchase so that, if we later add optional ads (for example a rewarded ad for an extra shuffle), players who bought ads-free keep that entitlement.

If we enable ads, they may be served by partners such as Google AdMob. Those partners may use advertising identifiers, IP address, and similar device data to serve, measure, and (where allowed) personalise ads. Where required, we will present a consent / privacy options flow (for example Google UMP). We will update this Policy before or when ads ship, and Play / App Store Data safety forms will be updated to match the build.

GameAnalytics may still use advertising or device identifiers for analytics even while no ads are shown. You can often reset or limit ad tracking in iOS or Android system settings.

6. Retention

  • Local save stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or use Delete account & data.
  • Cloud save and auth user are kept while your account exists, then removed when you delete the account (see below). Backups of our database may persist for a short period until they rotate.
  • Arcade scores tied to your player id are deleted with account deletion.
  • Analytics and crash reports are kept according to GameAnalytics and Sentry default retention (typically on the order of months, not indefinitely for our project). They may remain in aggregated, de-identified statistics.
  • Store purchase records are kept by Apple or Google under their policies. Deleting your Spellbun account does not cancel store purchases; you can Restore purchases after you play again.

7. Security

We use HTTPS for traffic to Supabase, GameAnalytics, and Sentry. Cloud save rows are limited so a signed-in player can only read or change their own save. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure. Please keep your Apple or Google account protected if you link it.

8. Your choices and rights

In the Game

  • Play without linking. Linking Apple or Google is optional. Unlinked progress may be lost if you uninstall or lose the anonymous session.
  • Delete account & data in Settings removes your cloud save, matching Arcade records, and local progress, signs you out, and starts a new anonymous session. Store purchases stay with Apple or Google.
  • Language can be switched between English and French in Settings.
  • System settings on your device can limit advertising identifiers, background data, and (on iOS) tracking permission.

Privacy law rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, or certain US states), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. To exercise rights that the in-app delete flow does not cover, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify the request (for example by confirming a linked account or player id) and we may refuse requests that are unlawful or would harm other players’ privacy.

If you are a California resident, we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are used in the CCPA/CPRA. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that require a right to limit under that law.

9. Children

Spellbun is a cute game, but it is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. In-app purchases (and possible future ads) are not intended for that age group. If you believe we have collected data from a child under 13, contact us and we will delete it. Parents and guardians should use device-level purchase controls.

10. International transfers

We and our providers may process data in countries other than yours (including the United States and the European Union). Those countries may have different data protection laws. Providers may use standard contractual clauses or other lawful transfer mechanisms where required.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy when the Game or the law changes (for example if we add ads or a new SDK). The “Last updated” date will change. Material changes will be reflected on this page; continued use after an update means you accept the revised Policy where permitted by law.

12. Contact

Privacy questions and data requests: open an issue on the public repository that hosts this page, KingSPi-GH/Spellbun-Docs, with enough detail for us to find your account (do not post secrets). For account deletion, use Delete account & data in Spellbun Settings; that is the primary and fastest path.
